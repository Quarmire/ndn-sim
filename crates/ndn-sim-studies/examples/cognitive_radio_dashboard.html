Listen-before-talk, under contention

Two simulated NDN nodes run the real RadioPolicy over a shared medium with a probabilistic per-frame erasure model. Delivery is noisy, MCS-adapted, averaged over 20 seeds — the experiment the tied-up hardware owed us, in software.

LBT recovers B: 0 → ~52 of 60

On a clean N=2 channel carrier-sense is useless (the on-air lesson). But add a co-band interferer that actually collides, and listen-before-talk earns its keep — node B goes from fully starved back to the link's natural ~87% by sensing the jammer and transmitting in the clear gap. The residual ~13% loss is the marginal link itself, not the interferer.

How to read the scopes

Each panel plots cumulative Data delivered over 60 rounds — a line that climbs steadily means every round got through. Node A's slot sits inside the interferer's burst; node B's is scheduled later. Watch B's teal line: it climbs in the baseline, flatlines at zero under the interferer with LBT off, and climbs again once LBT lets it back off into the clear gap.

Two regimes, one policy

Clean channel → carrier-sense only adds deferral latency (measured on-air: it even starves the weaker node). Collision-limited channel → it prevents the collisions that were the whole loss. Channel choice first; LBT when cornered.

Same brain as the radios

Each node's rate comes from the identical ndn-radio-cognition plane the LoRa/Wi-Fi hardware runs — fed the RSSI it hears off the bus. The sim decides exactly as the metal would, so N-large studies need no more antennas.

Fidelity & what this does not yet model

The medium is honest — a probabilistic per-frame erasure against a logistic PER curve (delivery falls as MCS is pushed past its SNR threshold), plus a deterministic hidden-terminal collision model. This scenario is link-level: it drives the bus directly with a scheduled turn-taking loop, not the full ForwarderEngine Interest→Data round-trip. So it faithfully models rate adaptation, contention, and carrier-sense — but not PIT aggregation, retransmission, or multi-hop forwarding. Those need the DES + real forwarder path (next). Numbers are averages over 20 seeds; the residual ~13% baseline loss is cognition's rate table sitting ~1 MCS above the sim PHY's optimum — a delivery-feedback learning loop would close it.
