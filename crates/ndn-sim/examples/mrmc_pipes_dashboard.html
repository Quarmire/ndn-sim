MRMC the named-data way: channel = H(name)

The doctrine correction that shaped this: in named-data radio the face is the medium — a channel-view of a shared broadcast medium, not a link to a peer. So multi-radio / multi-channel is not a host assigning a radio per hop (that's the IP-MRMC model we compare against). It's the medium spanning channels and content dispersing across them by hashing the name to a channel — no coordinator, no host radios. Measured here on real ForwarderEngine round-trips: eight named pipes in one collision domain, each rendezvousing on H(prefix) mod C.

A aggregate goodput vs channels
One channel collapses; spaced channels recover

On a single channel the eight pipes collide into congestion collapse. Hashing names across orthogonal channels recovers goodput super-linearly (the baseline is in collapse). Log scale — the climb spans a factor of ~35.

B airtime per delivered segment
Contention relief, in the airtime domain

Collisions make the medium re-spend airtime on retransmits. Orthogonal channels drive airtime-per-delivered toward the collision-free floor (~0.42×). Leaky channels still couple — so they stay expensive, and few pipes can even make C=2 worse than one channel.
