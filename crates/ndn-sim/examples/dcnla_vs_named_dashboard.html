ndn-pipes · #66 · thesis vs redesign

DCNLA vs named-data radio: two floors for the same pipe

The NDN-Pipes thesis (Le 2025) and the v2 redesign share a protocol — same messages, trust, teardown, and MRMC goal. They differ in the floor they build on, and only because the floor changed: named-data radio over monitor-mode Wi-Fi didn't exist when the thesis was written. So DCNLA got data-centric association the only way then available — by retooling host-centric Wi-Fi (IBSS, managed+hidden MAC/IP, coordinated channels). The redesign eliminates host identity outright. This compares what each floor buys and costs — quantified on the thesis's own MT7612U data.

THESIS · 2025

DCNLA — retool the host stack

Data-centric association layered on unmodified Wi-Fi: unicast over IBSS couplings, MAC/IP managed and hidden behind the name, per-segment channels coordinated by the LINK exchange, over NFD. "Does not modify how Wi-Fi works; retools its host-centric mechanisms."

REDESIGN · 2026

Named-data radio — remove the host

No association at all: monitor-mode broadcast, name-group hash as the L2 filter, ephemeral source nonce, channel = H(name). Nothing below the network layer holds a host identity; L2 is soft-state the forwarder can recompile.

A channel diversity · the MRMC crux
Guaranteed vs probabilistic

Both need adjacent hops on different channels (the thesis's whole point, Fig. 16). DCNLA's LINK coordinates it — adjacent segments are guaranteed to differ, so you always get the orthogonal case. Named-data's H(name) is stateless but only diverse in expectation; #71 measured the price when names collide onto adjacent channels.

B pipe setup cost
A handshake, or nothing

DCNLA stands a pipe up with a control handshake (SEEK→JOIN→CONTEXT→LINK→PIPE→CHECK) plus one IBSS association, then holds per-segment/coupling state. Named-data radio has no setup: the first Interest is broadcast on the name-group and forwarded by breadcrumb. State is the price of the guarantee.
