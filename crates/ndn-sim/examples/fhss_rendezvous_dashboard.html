Name-keyed FHSS: channel = hop(H(name), epoch)

#66 fixed the channel a name uses. #40 makes it hop. A flow's channel at epoch e is a name-shifted walk over #66's separated (non-adjacent) classes — computable by anyone holding the name and the shared epoch clock (#61's time-slice MAC / common-view TSF), so consumer, producer, and relays for /X rendezvous with no coordinator and no host identity. It's the frequency-domain sibling of the time-slice MAC. The payoff: a persistent jammer can kill a flow whose name statically hashes onto its channel — but a hopping flow only visits it 1/C of the time, so no flow is ever stranded. Measured on the real ForwarderEngine.
